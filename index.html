0vuln Range

A simulated hacking lab in a browser terminal: one target, ten services, ten flags. Every command generates real packets, and one command sends your session to PacketStory.

How I learn

Break it, look at it, then explain it to someone.

01 / break

Attack, legally

Hack The Box, TryHackMe, and labs I build myself. If I can't break it, I don't really understand it yet.

02 / see

Read the packets

Every attack leaves traffic behind. I go back and look at what my own attack looked like from the defender's chair.

03 / share

Teach it

My tools are free and open source, and I run CTFs for the ENSAK club. Explaining something is the real test.
